# Welcome to the mock-up of the Qahwatea Coffee website !! :)
## I'd love to give you some extra info on how this website works, and changes we could make in the future 

### This is a fully front-end, semi-interactive website.

## The home page contains features like:
    - header with logo and navigation bar that takes you to the home, menu, and contact pages
    - accessible address and hours of your establishment
    - photo gallery featuring your coffee and snacks that display their name when you hover, along with
    a button that brings you to the full menu page
    - a question form that brings you to a formatted Gmail setup for easy contact
    - a sendoff message with hand-drawn logos representing your company's values
    - footer with additional easy access to your Instagram, Facebook, and Gmail through icons that
    glow when you hover

## The menu page contains features like:
    - header and footer same as the home page
    - the main menu you've posted to Google
    - interactive items that feature your newly dropped items that aren't featured on the menu, with
    text encouraging visitors to keep updated with your Facebook and Instagram for new menu items (that
    link to your socials)

## The contact page contains features like:
    - header and footer same as the home/menu pages
    - very simple, aesthetic visualizations of all your contact information in one place for easy 
    accessibility, along with your hours once again, and encouragement to stop by for a visit
